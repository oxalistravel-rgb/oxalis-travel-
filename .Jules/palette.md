## 2024-03-21 - Viewport Zoom Accessibility
**Learning:** Preventing user zoom with `maximum-scale=1` or `user-scalable=no` in the viewport meta tag creates significant accessibility barriers for users with visual impairments who rely on zooming to read content.
**Action:** Always ensure the viewport meta tag only sets `width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0` and avoids restricting scaling capabilities.
