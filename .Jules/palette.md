## 2024-05-18 - Viewport Zoom Accessibility
**Learning:** Using `maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta tag restricts users from zooming in, which creates a significant accessibility barrier for visually impaired users who rely on zooming to read content.
**Action:** Always avoid setting a maximum-scale or setting `user-scalable=no` in viewport meta tags to ensure content remains accessible and scalable for all users.
